Free, in-person group counseling for adults, run by two trainee counselors from Universiti Malaya in collaboration with PT Foundation. Tell us you're interested. It takes about 2 minutes, and nothing is confirmed until we've spoken with you.

  1. Tell us you're interested

    This short form: which group, and how to reach you.

  2. Pre-screening call

    A 15–30 minute WhatsApp voice call from @imbrendonban (Brendon) or @ibentities (Benjamin), to get to know you and answer your questions. It isn't a therapy session.

  3. Register

    If the group is a good fit, we'll send you a short registration and consent form.

  4. Join the group

    We'll confirm your place, the schedule and the venue.
